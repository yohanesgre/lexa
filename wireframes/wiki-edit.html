Emberfall’s combat revolves around four elemental affinities: Fire, Ice, Earth, and Wind. Each enemy and boss has a hidden resistance matrix that determines damage multipliers.Caret

Damage Formula

Base damage is calculated from the weapon’s attack_power stat, modified by the player’s current affinity and the target’s resistance. The formula is intentionally simple — we want players to feel the impact of affinity choices without spreadsheet math. 

func calculate_damage(attack_power: int, affinity: String, target: Enemy) -> int:
    var base = attack_power * player.affinity_bonus(affinity)
    var resistance = target.resistance[affinity]
    var multiplier = 1.0 + (1.0 - resistance) * 2.0
    return int(base * multiplier)

Status Effects

Each element applies a unique status effect on critical hits (20% base chance, modifiable by gear):

  • Burn (Fire): 3 ticks of 15% base damage over 4 seconds. Stacks refresh duration.
  • Freeze (Ice): Target movement speed reduced 60% for 3 seconds. Bosses immune.
  • Petrify (Earth): 1.5 second stun. Breaks on damage taken. 30s cooldown per target.
  • Gale (Wind): Knocks back small enemies. Launches player upward if self-cast (mobility tool).
